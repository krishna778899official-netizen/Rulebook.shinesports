IMPORTANT NOTICE

This is a SHIN ESPORTS tournament rulebook. Weapon formats, equipment restrictions and match procedures below are tournament-specific rules. Management will apply the rules consistently and review violations using available evidence.

01

General Tournament Rules

Rules applicable to every registered participant

  1. Every registered player must follow this rulebook throughout the tournament.
  2. Players are responsible for understanding all tournament rules before participating.
  3. Fair play, sportsmanship and professional conduct are mandatory.
  4. Any attempt to gain an unfair advantage over an opponent is strictly prohibited.
  5. Players must follow all instructions issued by SHIN ESPORTS management.
  6. Management reserves the right to investigate suspected rule violations.
  7. Lack of knowledge of a rule will not be accepted as an excuse for violating it.
02

Match Format & Weapon Rules

Designated weapons for regular rounds and the Final

A. Matches Before the Final

  1. All matches before the Final will be played using M416 only.
  2. Players must use the designated weapon throughout the match.
  3. Using any other primary weapon is prohibited.

B. Final Match Format

The Final will consist of three separate matches:

  1. Each weapon must be used in its designated Final match.
  2. Players cannot replace the designated weapon with another primary weapon.

C. Pistol Restrictions

  1. Only Scorpion is permitted as a secondary pistol throughout the tournament.
  2. Every other pistol is strictly prohibited.
03

Equipment Restrictions

Prohibited protective equipment

  1. Level 3 helmets are strictly prohibited throughout the entire tournament.
  2. Level 3 vests are strictly prohibited throughout the entire tournament.
  3. These restrictions apply to every match, including the Final and any additional rematches.
  4. Players must not intentionally equip or use prohibited Level 3 equipment.
  5. Any equipment violation will be reviewed by management.
  6. The penalty will be decided by management based on available evidence, the circumstances and the seriousness of the violation.
Reminder: The Level 3 helmet and Level 3 vest restriction applies to the entire tournament, not only the regular rounds.
04

Match Draw & Tie-Breaker System

The exact order used when a match ends in a draw

Step 1 — Kill/Death Combo

  1. Management will compare both players' Kill/Death Combo results.
  2. The player with the better result will be declared the winner.
  3. If both players have identical Kill/Death Combo results, the next tie-breaker will apply.

Step 2 — Finish/Death Ratio (F/D Ratio)

  1. If both players have identical Kill/Death Combo results, their Finish/Death Ratios will be compared.
  2. The player with the higher F/D Ratio will be declared the winner.
  3. If both players have identical F/D Ratios, an additional rematch will be conducted.

Step 3 — Additional Rematch

  1. The additional rematch will have a duration of exactly 5 minutes.
  2. The first player to reach 3 kills within 5 minutes will immediately be declared the winner.
  3. If neither player reaches 3 kills within 5 minutes, the player with the higher kill count at the end of the rematch will win.
  4. If both players have equal kills when the 5-minute duration ends, the player who secured the last kill will be declared the winner.
  5. All applicable weapon and equipment restrictions will remain in force during the rematch.
Tie-breaker order: Kill/Death Combo → F/D Ratio → 5-minute rematch → first to 3 kills → higher kills at time limit → last kill.
05

POV & Handcam Requirements

Mandatory recording and evidence procedure

A. Standard POV Recording

  1. POV recording is mandatory for every player.
  2. Recording must begin before opening BGMI.
  3. Players must open BGMI directly through the official Google Play Store using the Open button.
  4. The recording must clearly show the game launching and the player joining the assigned custom room.
  5. Recording must continue until the player successfully enters the match.
  6. No cuts, edits, pauses, transitions or missing footage are permitted during this required process.
  7. After successfully entering the match, the player may stop the initial POV recording.
  8. Submitted recordings must be original and unaltered.

B. Handcam Recording

  1. Players who cannot record gameplay POV must provide proper handcam footage.
  2. Handcam must clearly show the mobile device and the player's hands throughout the entire gameplay.
  3. Players using handcam must also provide the initial POV recording.
  4. The initial recording must clearly show opening Google Play Store, opening BGMI directly through the Play Store, joining the assigned custom room and successfully entering the match.
  5. No cuts, edits, pauses or missing footage are permitted in the initial recording.
  6. After entering the match, players may stop the initial POV recording and continue with handcam.
  7. Handcam must remain active throughout gameplay.
  8. Handcam footage must not be interrupted, hidden or edited.

C. Recording Submission

  1. Players must submit their required recordings whenever requested by management.
  2. Players must preserve original recordings until the tournament and any pending investigation are completed.
  3. Failure to provide valid required footage may result in a penalty or disqualification.

06

Anti-Cheat & Prohibited Activities

Competitive integrity and fair-play protections

The following activities are strictly prohibited:

  1. Use of hacks, cheats, scripts, macros, bots or unauthorized third-party software.
  2. Use of modified game clients or unauthorized game files.
  3. Use of unauthorized hardware or devices that provide an unfair gameplay advantage.
  4. Exploiting bugs, glitches or unintended game mechanics to gain an advantage.
  5. Playing using another person's account or allowing another person to play using their account.
  6. Intentionally manipulating match results.
  7. Colluding with an opponent or deliberately throwing a match.
  8. Providing false information or manipulated evidence during an investigation.
  9. Any other activity that compromises competitive integrity or fair play.

Confirmed violations may result in match forfeiture or disqualification.

07

Suspicious Activity & Device Investigation

Evidence review, Discord inquiry and live verification

A. Initial Review

  1. If a player's gameplay appears suspicious, management may request and review the player's POV or handcam footage.
  2. Management may compare gameplay recordings and other available evidence.
  3. A player may be called for a Discord inquiry if management is not satisfied with the available evidence.
  4. Discord inquiry may also be conducted when a player has failed to follow mandatory POV or handcam requirements.

B. Device Investigation

  1. SHIN ESPORTS management reserves the right to request a device investigation whenever necessary.
  2. Device investigation may be requested even if the player has submitted proper POV and handcam recordings.
  3. Device verification will be conducted through Discord screen sharing and live device verification.
  4. Players must cooperate with reasonable verification instructions.
  5. Players cannot refuse an investigation simply because they have already submitted recordings.
  6. Refusal to cooperate, deliberate obstruction, hiding relevant evidence or misleading investigators may result in disqualification.
  7. Management will consider the available evidence before reaching its decision.

C. Investigation Confidentiality

  1. Investigation evidence, recordings and findings must not be deliberately manipulated or misrepresented.
  2. Players must not publicly circulate another player's private device information or personal data.
Investigation authority: Management may request a device investigation even when the submitted POV or handcam appears complete. Verification must be conducted through the stated Discord screen-sharing and live-verification process.
08

Player Conduct & Fair Play

Expected behaviour during the tournament

  1. Players must maintain respectful behaviour towards opponents, management and other participants.
  2. Abusive language, harassment, threats and personal attacks are prohibited.
  3. Players must not intentionally disturb or interfere with an ongoing match.
  4. Players must not attempt to influence opponents through threats, bribery or unfair agreements.
  5. Match fixing, intentional loss and result manipulation are strictly prohibited.
  6. Players must follow reasonable instructions issued by tournament officials.
  7. False accusations, fabricated evidence and deliberately misleading reports may result in disciplinary action.

10

Complaints & Dispute Resolution

How players must raise match-related concerns

  1. Players must report match-related issues directly to tournament management.
  2. Complaints should include relevant evidence wherever available.
  3. Players must not deliberately delay matches through baseless complaints.
  4. Management may review POV, handcam, match results and other available evidence.
  5. Players must cooperate during dispute resolution.
  6. A player cannot independently declare a match result invalid.
  7. Management will communicate the final decision to the concerned players.
11

Management Authority & Final Decisions

Administration and enforcement of the rulebook

  1. SHIN ESPORTS management is responsible for enforcing this rulebook.
  2. Management has the authority to review suspected violations and conduct investigations.
  3. Management may request additional evidence or device verification whenever necessary.
  4. Management may issue warnings, penalties, match forfeitures or disqualifications depending on the violation.
  5. Any situation not specifically covered in this rulebook will be evaluated using fair-play and competitive-integrity principles.
  6. All players must comply with reasonable tournament-related decisions.
  7. Management's final decision regarding tournament rule enforcement and match disputes will be binding within this tournament.
